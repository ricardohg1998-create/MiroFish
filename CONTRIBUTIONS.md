# My contributions to MiroFish

This is Ricardo Huertas's fork of [666ghj/MiroFish](https://github.com/666ghj/MiroFish). I use it to contribute to the original project. MiroFish itself is the work of its upstream authors and contributors.

## Spanish (Spain) localisation

I shared a Spanish (Spain) catalogue adapted to the current 635 locale entries, with placeholder checks and a frontend build. I posted the contribution on an existing Spanish-localisation pull request rather than opening a duplicate proposal.

[Contribution on PR #824](https://github.com/666ghj/MiroFish/pull/824#issuecomment-6145334714)

## Simulation completion and interview lifecycle

A simulation can finish its rounds while its process stays alive to serve interviews. Waiting for that process to exit before reporting completion leaves the UI waiting. I shared patches and tests that distinguish finished rounds, memory-write completion and the availability of the interview environment, plus a complementary close-environment improvement.

[Contribution on PR #846](https://github.com/666ghj/MiroFish/pull/846#issuecomment-6145335333)
